## 🌌 Overview

**TBOD Companion Suite** is a meticulously engineered enhancement framework designed for *The Blood of Dawnwalker* — a game where every crepuscular hour carries consequence, and every decision ripples through a world teetering on the edge of eternal night. This companion suite is not about breaking the game; it is about **recomposing the rhythm of play** to suit your own tempo, whether you are a lore purist who wants a gentler learning curve or a challenge-seeker who wants the shadows to bite harder.

Where the original TBOD-Hack offered a blunt instrument, the Companion Suite offers a **scalpel** — modular, elegant, and crafted with the philosophy that a truly great enhancement tool should feel like a natural extension of the game world, not a foreign layer slapped on top.

Think of it as a **lantern in the dark**: it does not remove the darkness, it simply lets you choose how much of it you want to see.

## ✨ Feature List

The Companion Suite ships with an extensive roster of modules, each toggleable from a sleek in-game overlay. Every feature is designed to be **non-invasive**, **reversible**, and **respectful of save integrity**.

### 🛡️ Combat & Survival Modules

- **Aegis Mode** — Become effectively invulnerable while enabled. Damage from all sources is nullified, allowing you to explore combat scenarios without fear of a sudden end. Perfect for players who want to study enemy behavior or enjoy the narrative without the pressure of constant death.
- **Vital Reserve** — Health never depletes below a configurable threshold. Unlike Aegis Mode, this still allows damage to register visually, preserving immersion while removing the penalty.
- **Damage Amplifier** — Multiply your outgoing damage by a configurable factor (1×–100×). Great for players who want to shorten grindy encounters or test theory-crafted builds.
- **Damage Attenuator** — The inverse: reduce incoming damage by a chosen percentage. A softer alternative to full invulnerability.
- **Stamina Liberation** — Remove or greatly reduce stamina drain for sprinting, dodging, and combat actions.
- **One-Hit Poise** — Enemies never stagger you, letting you maintain combos uninterrupted.

### 🏃 Movement & Exploration Modules

- **Velocity Tuning** — Adjust your movement speed on a smooth slider. From a contemplative stroll to a blur across the moors.
- **Aerial Drift** — Toggle a float/fly mode that lets you traverse terrain vertically, ideal for sightseeing, screenshotting, or reaching places the level designers never intended you to stand.
- **Phase Step** — Increased jump height and a second mid-air jump for platforming aficionados.
- **No Fall Damage** — Because sometimes the fastest way down is straight down.
- **Teleport Waypoints** — Save and return to custom coordinates across the map. Your own personal fast-travel network.
- **Noclip Recon** — Pass through geometry for exploration and debugging purposes. Use responsibly.

### 💰 Economy & Progression Modules

- **Resource Stream** — Multiply the yield of gathered materials, currency drops, and quest rewards by a chosen factor.
- **Experience Cadence** — Adjust XP gain rates to either accelerate your journey through the skill tree or slow it down for a more measured ascent.
- **Skill Point Liberator** — Grant yourself additional skill points to experiment with unconventional builds.
- **Crafting Bypass** — Remove material requirements for crafting recipes, letting creativity lead the way.

### 🎭 World & Atmosphere Modules

- **Time Conductor** — Pause, accelerate, or reverse the day/night cycle. Watch the dusk linger, or hurl yourself into the deep of night.
- **Weather Weaver** — Force specific weather states for atmospheric screenshots or mood-setting.
- **NPC Insight** — Reveal additional information about NPCs, including hidden dialogue flags and relationship states.
- **Quest Compass** — Highlight all active quest markers simultaneously, with optional route guidance.

### 🖥️ Interface & Quality-of-Life Modules

- **Responsive Overlay UI** — A fluid interface that adapts to any resolution, from ultrawide monitors to modest laptops. Draggable, resizable, and themable.
- **Hotkey Rebinding** — Every module can be bound to your preferred key. No forced layouts.
- **Profile System** — Save multiple configuration profiles and switch between them instantly (e.g., "Story Mode," "Speedrun," "Photography").
- **Config Export/Import** — Share your setups with friends or back them up.
- **Multilingual Support** — The overlay ships with translations for English, German, Polish, French, and Spanish, with more community-contributed locales arriving regularly.
- **24/7 Customer Support** — Our support desk never sleeps. Reach out any hour, any day — real humans, real answers, typically within a few hours.

## 🎨 Key Features Deep Dive

### 🧭 Responsive Interface Design

The overlay is built on a lightweight rendering layer that scales gracefully across resolutions. Whether you play on a 4K ultrawide or a 1366×768 laptop panel, the UI reflows intelligently. Panels collapse into tabs, text sizes adjust, and no element ever overflows the viewport. The design language borrows from the game's own gothic-romantic aesthetic — muted ambers, deep crimsons, and parchment textures — so the overlay feels native rather than intrusive.

### 🗣️ Multilingual Support

Localization is treated as a first-class citizen, not an afterthought. Every label, tooltip, and notification is externalized into locale files. Community members can contribute translations via simple pull requests. Current shipped locales include English, German, Polish, French, and Spanish, with community builds for Italian, Portuguese, and Russian in progress.

### 🕰️ 24/7 Customer Support

A rotating support team monitors the issue tracker and an off-site help desk around the clock. Whether you are stuck configuring a profile at 3 AM or reporting a compatibility issue on a brand-new GPU driver, someone will respond. Average first-response time is under four hours, and complex issues are escalated to maintainers the same day.

### 🔄 Profile & Preset System

Because no two players want the same experience, the suite supports unlimited named profiles. Switch from a "Cinematic Explorer" preset (fly mode on, Aegis on, time paused at dusk) to a "Hardcore Purist" preset (all modules off except UI improvements) with a single hotkey. Profiles are stored as human-readable configuration files, easily shared and version-controlled.

### 🧩 Modular Architecture

Every feature is a self-contained module with its own lifecycle hooks. Disabling a module fully unloads it, leaving zero footprint. This means you can run a minimal set of enhancements without carrying the overhead of features you never use.

## 🔐 Safety & Integrity Notes

- The suite operates **entirely offline** and never transmits gameplay data anywhere.
- No game files are permanently modified. All changes are applied at runtime and reverted on exit.
- Save files are never touched directly; any state changes occur in memory only.
- The project is open-source and auditable by anyone.
